Cookie policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with the "Cookie settings" button in the footer of the main page.

1. What cookies are

A cookie is a small file that a website stores in your browser. The law (Art. 22.2 LSSI-CE) also covers similar technologies such as the browser's local storage (localStorage and sessionStorage), which is what this website uses.

2. What we use

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies and does not set third-party tracking cookies. It only stores in your browser the technical data it needs to work or to do what you ask (remember your language, alias or votes). Under the LSSI-CE and the AEPD's cookie guidelines, these uses do not require consent.

The only thing that depends on your consent is Google Fonts (the "Preferences" category): if you do not accept it, the site uses your system fonts and does not connect to Google.

The banner also includes "Analytics" and "Marketing" categories in case tools of that kind are added in the future. Today they load nothing. If any are ever added, they will be listed in this table and only switched on if you accept them.

4. How to accept, reject or configure

The first time you visit, a banner shows three equally visible options: Accept all, Reject all and Settings (by category). Until you choose, only the technical items work. Your choice is kept for 12 months, after which we will ask again.

You can change your mind at any time from "Cookie settings" in the footer of the main page. You can also delete everything the site has stored from your browser settings, under site data.
